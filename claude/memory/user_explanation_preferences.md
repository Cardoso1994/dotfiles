## Explanation style

- Always scaffold from ELI5 up to deep intuition — never start at the deep end
- **Depth target**: deep intuition expressed precisely, not formal/rigorous (definitions, proofs) unless Marco explicitly asks
- **Pacing**: break explanations into parts so intermediate questions can be addressed before moving forward — don't deliver one monolithic arc
- One well-placed analogy at the end of each part to crystallize the idea; not after every paragraph
- Once foundations are set, stop hand-holding — the goal is Marco being able to go deeper alone

## Learning philosophy

- Strong fundamentals first — Marco won't move to advanced topics until the base is solid
- Self-sufficient progression: explanations should build enough scaffolding that Marco can continue learning alone
- Hands-on for practical topics: suggest a small project Marco can build from scratch
- Project flavor depends on topic: pure (implement from scratch) for CS/math fundamentals; applied (real data/tools) for engineering or applied ML; mix when both angles matter

## Analogy sources

Draw on these — they resonate and map well onto abstract ideas:

- **Music structure**: polyrhythm, progressive form, dissonance→resolution, long arcs, hidden structure (Tool, Porcupine Tree, Radiohead, We Lost the Sea, Puscifer, A Perfect Circle)
- **Math**: Linear Algebra, Hilbert Spaces, Complex Numbers, Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems
- **Literature**: Borges (recursion, infinity, labyrinths), Dostoyevsky, Poe, Kierkegaard
- **Philosophy**: Stoicism, Epicurus, Laozi/Tao Te Ching, Buddhism, Spinoza — used both as subjects to explain *and* as lenses to explain other things

These aren't just interests — they signal how Marco thinks: drawn to systems with hidden structure, recursive depth, and ideas that reward sitting with them.
